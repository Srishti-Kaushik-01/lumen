How do SANA diffusion models work?

How it works

SANA models are linear Diffusion Transformers built for fast, resource-efficient text-to-image generation. [1] They start from random noise and remove it step by step, using a simplified attention mechanism so that high-resolution images can be produced on modest hardware. [1] Later versions distil this process so that an image can be generated in as few as one to four steps. [2]

[1] SANA: Efficient High-Resolution Image Synthesis with Linear Diffusion TransformersEnze Xie et al. · 2025 · OpenAlex
[2] SANA-Sprint: One-Step Diffusion with Continuous-Time Consistency DistillationJunsong Chen et al. · 2025 · OpenAlex

How it works

Each question goes through the same transparent pipeline.

Ask

Type a research question. Short follow-ups are resolved against the earlier questions in the conversation.

Retrieve and rank

Lumen searches OpenAlex and a curated collection, removes duplicates, and ranks candidates with semantic embeddings.

Get a written answer

A language model writes a clear answer from the ranked papers, cites each claim, and quotes the key evidence beneath it.

Questions

Where do the answers come from?

From abstracts of papers returned by OpenAlex and from a small curated collection. A language model writes the answer from those texts and cites each claim; quoted evidence is shown so you can check it.

What happens if no paper answers my question?

Lumen still answers from general knowledge, but says clearly that no paper supported it and that the answer has no citations.

Is my research private?

Yes. Conversations are stored against your account only. Only a short keyword query is sent to OpenAlex, and you can delete conversations or your whole account.

Does it need a GPU or a paid API?

No. The language model is a compact pretrained transformer that runs on an ordinary CPU, and every service it uses is free.
